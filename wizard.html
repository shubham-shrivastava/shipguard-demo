Travel policy

Read the policy to the end to enable the acknowledgement.

1. Booking

All bookings must be made through the corporate travel desk at least seven days before departure. Bookings made outside the desk are reimbursed only with prior written approval from the cost-center owner.

2. Class of travel

Flights under six hours are economy. Flights over six hours may be premium economy with manager approval. Rail is first class where the fare difference is under 20 percent.

3. Accommodation

Hotel rates must not exceed the city cap published on the intranet. Extended-stay rates apply to trips longer than fourteen nights.

4. Expenses

Meals are reimbursed at actuals up to the daily cap with itemised receipts. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Ground transport should prefer public transit or shared rides.

5. Changes and cancellations

Changes after ticketing require the travel desk. No-shows without notice are charged to the traveller's department and reviewed quarterly.
